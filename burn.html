Franchise Spotlight

jpwilli1 (Commissioner)
Waiver #7, dead center of the pack, which is exactly where the person who writes the league rules and sets the waiver clock should be terrified to land. Nothing says "impartial" like finishing average in your own league.
Goal for the season: prove that "commissioner" isn't a synonym for "guy who's good at bylaws, bad at football."
Shit diarrhea Sandwhich (treyday89)
Owns the league's biggest taxi squad (4 stashes) and, somehow, an even bigger commitment to the bit that is this team name. This is a front office playing four years down the road while the current roster is held together with waiver-wire tape.
Goal for the season: lose as many games as possible with dignity, and let the taxi squad ferment into something edible by 2028.
HomemadeSalsa
Waiver priority #2, which in this league means "recently one of the worst teams alive," paired with a player already parked on IR before Week 1 even kicked off. The recipe needs work.
Goal for the season: stop the bleeding, salvage two or three usable ingredients, and pray the pico de gallo isn't actually just regret.
bltzkriegbalers
Dead last in waiver priority (#1) with zero players stashed on the taxi squad, meaning this team has no future assets banked and no recent success to show for it either. All gas, no plan, no parachute.
Goal for the season: win now because there is, quite literally, no later to punt to.
MitchC23
Comfortably, forgettably middle-of-the-pack (waiver #4), running a roster with one man down on IR and three taxi stashes quietly cooking in the background. The definition of "fine."
Goal for the season: retool on the fly without anyone noticing you were ever rebuilding at all.
TornBrownsFan
A name that is either a Cleveland Browns fan or a description of this roster after this season, and possibly both. Sitting at waiver #3 with three taxi squad kids stashed like emergency rations.
Goal for the season: brace for the inevitable, root for the rookies, accept that suffering built character (it did not; it built a losing record).
BloneSack
Two players already on Injured Reserve before a single snap of real football has been played this year. That's not bad luck, that's a pattern.
Goal for the season: survive the trainer's room, hope the taxi squad depth (2 stashed) is enough to paper over the injury report by Week 8.
MrWorldbeater
A name that promises global domination, backed up by a healthy roster (zero IR, zero taxi stashes) and a high waiver slot (#11) suggesting recent success. No prospects stashed means no excuses either.
Goal for the season: live up to the username or change it — there is no in-between here.
BlueRoofBandits
Riding high waiver priority (#12) off presumably recent success, but already running two players on IR before kickoff. Bandits who stole a good record last year and are now getting robbed by the training staff.
Goal for the season: outrun the injury bug long enough to steal one more playoff spot.
Plums (damocon)
The league's other max-taxi-squad team (4 stashes) with an IR body already on the shelf, quietly building a juggernaut for a season that isn't this one. Drawing jpwilli1 in Week 1 is either a gift or a trap depending on how you feel about the commissioner.
Goal for the season: treat 2026 as a bridge year and let the fruit ripen for 2027.
ChaseMode1
A name built entirely around perpetually being one step behind, sitting at waiver #10 with a clean, fully-active roster (no IR, no taxi). No excuses, no prospects to hide behind.
Goal for the season: finally catch up to whoever you've been chasing and lead a division for once.
jimpjimp
Dead last in the waiver order in the good way: pick #14 means this roster was presumably the class of the league recently, and with zero taxi stashes and zero IR bodies, it's all-in on right now.
Goal for the season: go back-to-back, because a roster this loaded has zero excuses for anything less.
itsTHEkenny
Solidly mid-table on paper (waiver #6), fully healthy, no taxi squad to speak of. The quiet, unassuming team nobody's talking about, which is exactly how sleeper (pun intended) seasons start.
Goal for the season: prove last year's finish wasn't a fluke and sneak into the playoffs while everyone else is busy roasting each other.
liamgranty
An elite waiver slot (#13, near the top of the standings pecking order) completely undercut by THREE players already on Injured Reserve before the season has even started. This is a Ferrari with three flat tires.
Goal for the season: nurse the roster back to health fast enough to cash in a genuinely good draft/waiver position before it goes to waste.
