Art. 1 League Overview

1.1 League Name

The league shall be known as "Franchise Mode."

1.2 League Format

Franchise Mode is a 14-team Superflex Dynasty League.

1.3 Platform

The league is hosted and operated on Sleeper.

1.4 Standings Format

Franchise Mode uses a hybrid head-to-head plus league-median scoring format. Each week, in addition to a franchise's standard head-to-head matchup, that franchise's score is also compared against the league median score for that week. As a result, a franchise may earn two wins in a single week (a head-to-head win plus a median win), one win, or zero wins, depending on results.

Art. 2 Season Calendar

2.1 Regular Season

The regular season runs from Week 1 through Week 14.

2.2 Playoffs

The championship playoffs run from Week 15 through Week 17.

2.3 Trading Window

Trading is open year-round, with the following exceptions:

  • Trading is closed entirely during the startup draft.
  • Trading is closed during Weeks 14 through 17.

2.4 FAAB Reset

FAAB budgets reset to $100 per franchise annually, following the conclusion of the real-world NFL Draft. Any bonus FAAB earned through the Franchise Tag System (Article 7) or the Rivalry Cup (Article 12) is added on top of the standard reset amount at that same time.

Transparency note: the highest a franchise's budget can start a season at, stacking every possible bonus at once, is $165 — $100 base, plus a refunded Legend-tier deposit (+$30, Article 7.5), plus the Legend tier benefit (+$25, Article 7.3), plus a Rivalry Cup bonus (+$10, Article 12.5). Reaching this ceiling requires five consecutive seasons tagging the same player and a rivalry sweep in that same season — an intentionally rare combination, and one that resets fully the following year.

Art. 3 League Dues & Buy-In

3.1 Standard Buy-In

The league buy-in is $30 per franchise, per season.

3.2 Upfront Commitment

At league launch, each franchise pays 2 seasons of dues upfront, for a total of $60.

3.3 Ongoing Payment Structure

Following the initial launch, the league operates on a "pay for the season ahead" model — dues for the upcoming season are collected during or by the end of the current season, ensuring the league remains funded one season in advance at all times.

Art. 4 Startup Draft

4.1 Format — 3rd Round Reversal

The startup draft follows a 3rd Round Reversal format: Rounds 1 and 2 follow standard snake order. Round 3 repeats the same direction as Round 2 (rather than reversing again, as a standard snake draft would). Standard snake order resumes for all rounds thereafter.

This format prevents the owner who picks last in Round 1 from also picking near-immediately again at the top of Round 3.

4.2 No Trading

No trades of any kind are permitted during the startup draft. Trading opens immediately once the startup draft concludes.

4.3 Draft Order

The startup draft order is randomized three (3) times. The final (third) randomization stands as the official draft order.

Art. 7 Franchise Tag System

7.1 Purpose

The Franchise Tag exists to reward loyalty between a franchise and its players, allowing each owner to designate a "franchise player" each season.

7.2 Tagging Rules

  • A franchise may have only one tagged Franchise Player at a time.
  • The tagged player does not need to be the same player each season — but tagging the same player across consecutive seasons builds tier status and benefits.
  • If a franchise does not tag a player in a given season, they carry no tagged player that season and their consecutive-tag streak resets to 0.
  • Tagging Deadline: all franchise tags must be submitted by the end of the weekend before the NFL regular season kicks off.
  • To receive any benefit for a given season's tag, the tagged player must remain on the franchise's roster for the entire season.

7.3 Tier System

TierConsecutive SeasonsBenefit
Bronze1 seasonNo benefit
Silver2 consecutive seasons+$10 FAAB at season start
Gold3–4 consecutive seasons+$20 FAAB at season start
Franchise Legend5 consecutive seasons+$25 FAAB at season start

Bonus FAAB is added annually at the same time as the standard FAAB reset (Article 2.4).

7.4 Milestone Comp Picks

  • Gold Milestone (Year 3): reaching Gold tier in Year 3 awards the franchise a 4th round rookie comp pick in the following rookie draft.
  • Legend Milestone (Year 5): reaching Franchise Legend in Year 5 awards the franchise a 3rd round rookie comp pick in the following rookie draft.
  • Milestone Requirement: to receive a comp pick, the tagged player must (a) remain on the franchise's roster the entire season, and (b) start a minimum of 7 games, if healthy enough to do so.

7.5 Tag Cost & Season Deposit

Continuing a Franchise Tag streak into a new tier requires the tagging owner to pay a FAAB deposit at the tagging deadline (Article 7.2). Deposits are only charged when crossing into a new tier — holding within the same tier (e.g., Year 4 of Gold) costs nothing further:

TransitionDeposit
Year 1 (Bronze)Free
Year 2 → Silver$10 FAAB
Year 3 → Gold$20 FAAB
Year 4 (staying Gold)Free
Year 5 → Legend$30 FAAB

The deposit is refunded in full at the end of the season if the tagged player remains on the franchise's roster for the entire season, satisfying Article 7.2's full-season requirement. If the player is dropped or traded away before season's end, the deposit is forfeited — in addition to the penalties in Article 7.6 or 7.7. The deposit and any tier benefit (Article 7.3) are independent of one another; both apply in full when earned.

7.6 Dropping a Tagged Player — Penalty

If an owner drops their tagged Franchise Player mid-season, breaking the full-season roster requirement, the owner (a) forfeits that season's tag deposit under Article 7.5, if one was paid, and (b) pays an additional FAAB penalty based on the player's tier at time of drop. These two costs stack.

7.7 Trading Away Your Own Tagged Player — Penalty

If an owner trades away their own tagged Franchise Player mid-season, breaking the full-season roster requirement, the trading-away owner (a) forfeits that season's tag deposit under Article 7.5, if one was paid, and (b) pays the same FAAB penalty scale as Article 7.6, based on the player's tier at time of trade:

Tier Traded AwayPenalty

This penalty is in addition to — not in place of — the trading-away owner's right to receive the acquiring team's compensation under Article 7.8.

7.8 Trading a Tagged Player — Acquisition Cost

The acquiring team owes the trading-away team additional compensation, on top of the trade itself, based on tier:

TierBuyer Pays Seller

7.9 Tag Reset on Acquisition

A tagged player acquired via trade does not bring their tier or streak with them — their consecutive-tag streak resets to 0 upon joining the new franchise. The acquiring team may not tag that player until the next tagging deadline, at which point a fresh streak may begin.

7.10 Record-Keeping

Current tag status is documented via Commissioner Notes on the player in Sleeper. League Notes maintain a historical record of every player who has reached Legend status.

Art. 8 Taxi Squad

Rookie players are taxi-squad eligible for one (1) year — their rookie season, or first year on a roster. Each franchise carries 4 taxi squad spots (Article 5.2).

Art. 9 Waivers

9.1 Processing

Waivers are processed nightly at midnight, every night of the season.

9.2 Freeze Period

Waivers freeze following the Super Bowl and reopen after the conclusion of the annual rookie draft. The exact reopening date and time will be announced by the Commissioner each year.

Art. 10 Trades

10.1 Trade Processing

Trades process instantly, with no standard review or veto period. The sole exception is in cases of obvious collusion: the Commissioner reviews such cases first, and only in extreme cases does the matter escalate to a full league vote (majority of all 14 owners, per Article 17).

10.2 Trading Future Draft Picks

Trading away a future draft pick of any round, for any future season, obligates the trading-away owner to remain financially committed — i.e., current on league dues — through each season those traded picks belong to.

Example: if an owner trades away their 2028 4th round pick and their 2029 3rd round pick, that owner must be paid up through both the 2028 and 2029 seasons.

Art. 11 Lineup Compliance

Every franchise owner must set a legal starting lineup each week (all required starting slots filled with eligible players, no empty or illegal slots). Offenses are tracked per season and escalate as follows:

  1. 1st Warning
  2. 2nd $20 FAAB penalty
  3. 3rd Loss of the owner's next owned 3rd round rookie pick
  4. 4th Potential removal from the league, at the Commissioner's sole discretion — no league vote required

Art. 12 Rivalry Weeks

12.1 Designated Weeks

Week 6 and Week 13 are designated Rivalry Weeks.

12.2 Rival Selection

Following the startup draft, the Commissioner will go in reverse order of the startup draft and ask each franchise to select their rival, proceeding down the list until all rivalries are set.

12.3 Rivalry Cycle

Rivalries reset every 2 seasons. At the end of a 2-season cycle, franchises re-select rivals via the same reverse-order process — unless two rival franchises mutually agree to continue their existing rivalry for an additional cycle, in which case that pairing carries over.

12.4 Rivalry Matchups

Rival franchises play each other twice per season — once in Week 6 and once in Week 13 — regardless of the regular schedule otherwise generated.

12.5 Rivalry Rewards

  • Winning a Rivalry Week matchup awards the winning team $10 FAAB, usable only during the current season (does not roll over).
  • A franchise that wins both of its rivalry matchups in a season is awarded the Rivalry Cup, plus an additional $10 FAAB added to their FAAB budget for the following season (applied at the standard reset, per Article 2.4).

Art. 13 Playoffs, Seeding & Rookie Draft Order

13.1 Playoff Field

Eight (8) of the league's 14 teams qualify for the championship bracket.

13.2 Seeding

SeedsDetermined By
1 – 4Total wins (head-to-head + league median combined, per Article 1.4)
5 – 7Points For (PF)
8Max Points

13.3 Round 1 Matchup Selection

Once playoff qualifiers are announced, seeds select their own Round 1 opponent in seed order: #1 picks first, then #2, then #3, and so on, each selecting from the pool of remaining available opponents.

Each seed has a 12-hour window to make their selection once it becomes their turn; the clock starts for #1 as soon as qualifiers are announced. If a seed fails to select within their 12-hour window, they are automatically matched with the lowest remaining available seed.

13.4 Rookie Draft Order

  • Picks 7–14: determined by championship bracket finish, reversed — the team finishing 8th in the bracket receives pick 7, 7th place receives pick 8, continuing up to the champion (1st place finish) receiving pick 14.
  • Picks 1–6: determined by the 6 non-playoff teams, ordered by Max Points Forced — the worst-performing non-playoff team receives pick 1, and the best-performing non-playoff team receives pick 6.

Art. 14 Rookie Draft Format (Annual)

Beginning with the season following the startup draft, all annual rookie drafts use a Linear (Straight) format — the same draft order established per Article 13.4 repeats every round, rather than snaking.

Art. 15 Payouts

The total season payout pool is $420, sourced from the $30 per-team buy-in across all 14 franchises (Article 3.1).

The league treasury (funded separately via Article 16 replacement-owner fees) is held apart from the season payout pool and is used for additional prizes at the Commissioner's discretion.

Art. 16 Owner Departure & Franchise Replacement

If an owner leaves the league after having already prepaid dues for a future season, and a replacement owner takes over that franchise, the incoming owner pays half price to join (since the season is already funded). That half-price payment goes into the league treasury, to be used for additional prizes/payouts.

If an owner leaves the league without having prepaid for a future season, the replacement owner pays full price to join.

Art. 17 Rule Changes & Amendments

New proposed rules and ideas are put to a poll/vote during the offseason. A proposal passes with a majority vote of all 14 owners (8 or more votes required, regardless of turnout).

This constitution is a living document, subject to amendment per Article 17.
